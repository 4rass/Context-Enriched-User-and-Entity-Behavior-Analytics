01 / THE CHALLENGE

Unusual activity.
Incomplete picture.

A promotion. A late deployment. Access to a newly assigned server. Legitimate work can look suspicious when behavior is viewed alone.

False positives compete for an analyst’s attention. CE-UEBA explores how operational context can help explain these signals and focus the review on activity that remains suspicious.

The key question: What changed around this user?

Inside the score: what does “ML risk” mean?

ML stands for machine learning. In this demonstration, the initial ML risk scores are simulated values, not predictions from a trained model. Explicit rules then adjust those values on a 0–100 scale.

Approved changes and recent authorized role changes can lower risk. Inactive accounts, critical assets, and privileged actions can raise it. Suppression requires a score below 40, a strong legitimate factor, and no critical suspicious factors or inactive employment status. Department alignment alone cannot suppress an alert.
