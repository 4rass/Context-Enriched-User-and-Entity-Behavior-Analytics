How are scores and suppression decided?

Approved change −35 · recent authorized role change −20 · department alignment −10 · inactive employee +30 · critical asset +15 · privileged action +20. Scores stay between 0 and 100.

Low: below 30 · Medium: 30–59.9 · High: 60–79.9 · Critical: 80–100. Suppression requires a score below 40 and a strong legitimate factor, with no critical suspicious factors or inactive employee status.
